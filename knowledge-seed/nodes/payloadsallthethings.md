# PayloadsAllTheThings

**Summary:** A huge, well-organized repo of payloads, bypasses, and methodology
notes per vulnerability class — the go-to when you need a working payload or a
filter-bypass idea fast.

## URL
https://github.com/swisskyrepo/PayloadsAllTheThings

## Why it's worth it
- Per-class folders (SQLi, XSS, SSRF, XXE, SSTI, CSV injection, OAuth, JWT, etc.)
  with payloads, WAF/filter bypasses, and references.
- Pairs theory ([[portswigger-academy]]) with ready-to-fire payloads.
- Regularly updated by the community.

## Jump to for
- SSRF bypasses → feeds [[ssrf-cloud-metadata]].
- OAuth / redirect_uri tricks → feeds [[oauth-account-takeover]].
- SSTI, JWT, and upload bypass sections for quick wins.
